About EduTrack

Making student and academic management simpler.

Who We Are

EduTrack is a student performance and academic management platform designed to help schools and educational institutions manage student records, subject marks, attendance, and academic progress in one place. It brings essential academic information together in a clear and easy-to-use workspace.

Our Mission

Our goal is simple: to make academic management more organised, reliable, and accessible. We aim to reduce manual work, make student performance easier to understand, and help educational institutions focus more on teaching and learning.

Why Choose EduTrack?

  • All-in-One Academic Management: Manage student details, marks, attendance, and performance from one place.
  • Flexible Grading and Subjects: Configure grading settings and manage subjects to suit your institution.
  • Clear Academic Insights: Review student performance and export academic data when needed.
  • Simple and Practical: A straightforward interface built to make everyday academic tasks easier.
